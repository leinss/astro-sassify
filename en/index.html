Mission

Tobias believes businesses shouldn't spend their time on work a computer could do better. His mission is to help mid-sized companies identify automation opportunities and implement solutions that actually work – saving time, reducing errors, and freeing teams to focus on what matters. He combines technical expertise with a practical approach: every automation should have clear ROI and be maintainable by your team.

Experience

Tobias is a software engineer with deep experience in AI, blockchain, and full-stack development. He has worked at research institutions like Fraunhofer, built blockchain infrastructure in the Ethereum and Cosmos ecosystems, and developed AI-powered software solutions. As a co-founder of multiple startups and winner of numerous hackathons, he understands both the technical and business sides of automation. His focus is on building scalable systems, automating digital processes, and integrating modern tools into business operations.

Approach

Growing up in the Stuttgart region – the heart of German manufacturing and SMEs – Tobias understands the challenges mid-sized businesses face. These companies need to digitize and automate, but can't afford enterprise consultants or year-long implementation projects. His goal is to bring modern automation capabilities to businesses that are ready to grow but don't have dedicated IT teams for every initiative.

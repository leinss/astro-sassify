AI Invoice Reader

From PDF to booked data in seconds

Problem

Teams retype invoice data into accounting software by hand — slow, error-prone, and impossible to audit.

Solution

A workflow reads each invoice with a vision model, extracts vendor, amount, VAT and line items, validates them, and routes clean data onward — with a human-review path for anything uncertain.

Result

Invoice handling drops from about 4 hours a week to roughly 15 minutes.

Lightning Lead Response

Answer every lead in minutes, not hours

Problem

Leads that wait hours for a reply go cold, and enquiries that arrive after hours are lost overnight.

Solution

Each incoming lead is scored and answered with a personalised, on-brand reply within minutes, scheduled to business hours, and logged to your CRM with a follow-up task.

Result

Response time falls from around 6 hours to under 5 minutes — the window where leads are far more likely to convert.

Meeting Minutes Bot

Problem

Solution

Spreadsheet Rescue

From spreadsheet chaos to a clean system

Problem

Critical processes run on fragile spreadsheets — manual entry, version chaos, silent formula breaks, and no validation.

Solution

The workflow analyses your sheet, proposes a clean schema, then deduplicates, standardises and validates the data on its way into a proper system.

Result

Messy, copy-pasted tables become a structured, validated dataset you can trust.

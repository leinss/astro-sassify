Career

Tobias is an experienced software engineer with a focus on artificial intelligence, blockchain, and full-stack development. He has worked in various international companies and research institutions. His professional experience includes positions at the Fraunhofer Institute, blockchain projects in the Ethereum and Cosmos ecosystems, and the development of AI-powered software solutions. In addition, he was involved as a co-founder in innovative startups and has demonstrated his expertise through numerous successful participations in hackathons. His focus is on the development of scalable software solutions, the automation of digital processes, and the integration of AI into business operations. He is proficient in both backend and frontend technologies and has extensive experience in the areas of DevOps, data engineering, and software architecture.

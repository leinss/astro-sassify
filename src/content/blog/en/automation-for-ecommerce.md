> **Short answer:** For an online shop, the highest-payback automations are keeping inventory in sync across every sales channel, triaging support so common questions get instant answers, and sending order and shipping updates automatically. Inventory sync prevents overselling; support triage frees your team for the cases that need a human. Both start with one channel or one question type and expand from there.

E-commerce runs on volume, and volume is where manual work breaks. These are the processes worth automating first in a growing shop.

## The four highest-value targets

| Process | The pain | What automation does | Service |
|---------|----------|----------------------|---------|
| Multi-channel inventory sync | Overselling; stock drift across platforms | Real-time sync across shop, marketplaces, ERP | [Integrations & APIs](/en/services/integrations-apis/) |

## Start here: inventory sync

Selling the same stock on your own shop, a marketplace, and maybe a physical location means someone reconciles inventory by hand — and you oversell the moment they're behind.

Connect the channels so a sale anywhere updates stock everywhere in real time. The [multi-platform inventory sync case study](/en/blog/case-study-ecommerce-sync/) walks through this for a retailer, and it's core [integrations & APIs](/en/services/integrations-apis/) work.

## Then: triage support

Most support tickets are the same handful of questions — where's my order, how do I return this, do you ship to X. Your team answers them all day while the genuinely tricky cases wait.

A retrieval workflow answers the common ones instantly with sourced replies and a confidence score, and escalates anything uncertain to a person. The [support-assistant demo](/en/projects/) shows it live, and the [AI support triage case study](/en/blog/case-study-support-triage/) covers a shop processing 50k monthly orders.

## Order updates and cart recovery

- **Order & shipping updates:** trigger a status message at each step — confirmed, packed, shipped, delivered — so customers stop emailing "where's my order?" See [communication automation](/en/services/communication-automation/).
- **Cart recovery:** a timely, personalized nudge after an abandoned cart recovers revenue you already earned the click for. That's [CRM & sales automation](/en/services/crm-sales-automation/) territory — see [automating customer communication](/en/blog/automating-communication/).

## A realistic first project

1. Fix the one that's costing you money now — usually inventory sync if you sell on more than one channel.
2. Automate a single connection (shop ↔ marketplace) and prove it holds.
3. Add support triage for your top five questions.
4. Layer on order updates and cart recovery once the basics run themselves.

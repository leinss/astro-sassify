```
flat illustration style, pastel blue (#0ea5e9) and orange/peach color scheme, professional business setting, clean minimal design, no text, white background, modern corporate aesthetic
```

## Instructions

1. Open [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/app)
2. Copy each prompt and paste into Gemini
3. Download the generated image
4. Rename to the specified filename
5. Move to `src/assets/images/pillars/`

**Tip:** If a generation doesn't match the style, try regenerating or adjusting "isometric" / "flat 2D" / "abstract" keywords.
